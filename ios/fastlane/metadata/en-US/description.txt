MangaBaka is the official, free, and open-source companion app for MangaBaka.org.

Features:
- Track your reading progress across reading, completed, paused, plan to read, dropped, and rereading lists
- Browse and search the MangaBaka catalog with rich filters, genres, tags, and publisher info
- Scan ISBN barcodes with your camera to quickly look up physical volumes
- Organize and explore Stacks (curated lists)
- Import your existing library from AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, MangaUpdates, Comick, or Mihon
- Automatic cloud sync with your MangaBaka account and offline-first local caching
- Light, dark, and customizable theme accents with multilingual support

Note: MangaBaka is a manga tracking and discovery app. It does not host or stream copyrighted reading content.
